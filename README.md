#Problem Statement - Customer Segmentation & Retention Analysis

-> A UK-based online retailer selling giftware to both individual consumers and 
wholesale customers is experiencing inconsistent revenue growth despite a large 
and active customer base. Leadership has no systematic way to identify which 
customers drive the most value, which are at risk of disengaging, and how 
retention efforts should be prioritized across different customer types.

This project analyzes two years of transactional data (Online Retail II dataset, 
~1M+ transactions, 2009–2011) to build a data-driven customer segmentation model 
and a churn risk framework. The goal is to move the business from a 
"one-size-fits-all" marketing approach to targeted retention strategies — 
identifying high-value customers worth protecting, mid-tier customers worth 
nurturing, and at-risk segments that need intervention before they're lost.

#Dataset

Source:Online Retail II — UCI Machine Learning Repository
Size: ~1,067,371 transactions, 8 columns
Time period:December 2009 – December 2011
